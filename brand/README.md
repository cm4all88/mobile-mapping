## Notes on use

- Both files carry an alpha channel, so they sit on any background. They are drawn in
  dark charcoal and will disappear on a dark ground — use them on white or light only,
  or request a reversed version.
- The wordmark at 605 px wide is about 2 inches at 300 dpi. That is adequate for a
  document header but tight for a full-width cover treatment in print.

> **PARAMETRIX DECISION REQUIRED**
>
> Confirm three things with whoever owns the brand standard:
>
> 1. Whether a **vector** version (SVG, EPS, AI) exists. Vector would remove any
>    reproduction-size limit and is worth having before this SOP is printed or issued
>    at scale.
> 2. Whether a **reversed / light** version exists for dark backgrounds.
> 3. Minimum clear space and minimum reproduction size, and whether the wordmark or the
>    X mark is correct for an internal SOP cover.
>
> *Recommended practice:* wordmark on the cover and first page, X mark as a small
> repeating element in the running header. Request the vector files.
