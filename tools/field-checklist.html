1   Before leaving the office SOP §5, §6

  • Route, pass count and direction planned per pass
  • Satellite almanac checked — gnssplanning.com/#/charts
  • Two initialization locations identified — open sky, both ends
  • GNSS-hostile stretches mapped; strategy for each
  • Weather checked; stand-down criteria understood
  • Vehicle height clearances checked on route
  • Mechanical check — mounting, screws, torques
  • System settings checked — lever arms, sensor settings
  • SSDs prepared and field protocol printed
  • Cleaning kit aboard · Wi-Fi password sticker accessible
  • If air-freighted: 24 h acclimatisation complete
  • If idle > 2 months: run 30–60 min beforehand

2   Safety check — before AND after every mission SOP §6

Replace damaged parts immediately · tighten loose screws to correct torque · clean or dry anything wet
  • Roof rack installed correctly; all screws tight
  • Roof rack shows no cracks or deformation
  • Sensor Unit damage-free — no scratches or deformation
  • All camera lenses clean and undamaged
  • Sensor in operation position and properly locked
  • Control Unit undamaged, installed correctly, secured
  • Power Unit undamaged, secured, vents clear
  • All connectors plugged in and fixed
  • Cables fixed to roof rack before entering the cabin
  • No cable damaged or liable to be damaged
  • Operator device working
DO NOT START a mission with an unresolved issue from a previous mission. Trimble: “Not doing this may damage the system permanently.”

3   Power up — order matters SOP §7

  • 1Engine auto start/stop OFF
  • 2Both SSDs inserted and locked
  • 3START THE VEHICLE
  • 4Hold Control Unit power button ≥ 15 seconds
  • 5Power Unit LED constant green
  • 6SU + CU LEDs blink ~10 s → solid green
  • 7Connect tablet → Chrome → http://tmi.mx-scan.net
  • 8Wi-Fi country set (first use / new country)
LEDMeaning
Blinking greenStarting / updating / shutting down
Solid greenReady
Blinking redComponent failed — do not start a mission
WHY THE ORDER Startup draws 25 A. Powering up on a non-running engine is how you meet the Battery Protect cutoff the hard way.

4   System health before initializing SOP §7

  • Disk check complete — no Warning or Error icon
  • Correct Vehicle Preset — install height matches this vehicle today
  • DMI / GAMS checkboxes ACTIVATED if fitted
  • Correct Capture Preset selected
  • Camera and Laser buttons GREEN
  • UTC time showing · vehicle parked in open sky
SILENT FAILURE An unactivated DMI or GAMS logs nothing — even though it is fitted, measured and connected. Nothing in the field will tell you.

5   Initialization — the most important 20 minutes SOP §8

  • 1Parked open sky, good PDOP, clear of buildings
  • 2Mission started — name, area, presets confirmed
  • 3STILL for 2–3 minutes  — it feels long; it is not optional
  • 4Blue arrow visible; UTC time showing
  • 5Drive straight ~20 m → NAV RED → ORANGE
  • 6Vary speed + 2–3 dynamic turns → NAV ORANGE → GREEN
    Example: 0 → 50 → 20 → 50 → 20 km/h
  • 7All parameters green in Nav View
  • 8WAIT — up to 10 more minutes before logging
  • 9Initialization time and location recorded
NAVMeaningCan you record?
REDNo valid solution / navigation failureBlocked by the system
ORANGEA solution existsPermitted — but not for survey grade
GREENMeets your user accuracy figuresYes — this is the target
FIELD TIP Watch which parameter holds you at orange. Heading → more dynamic manoeuvres. Position → move to a better location; more circles won't help.

6   Navigation logging vs. recording — three rules SOP §8, §9

Starts automaticallyAs soon as a valid position exists and the blue arrow appears — even if NAV is still red
Unaffected by colourOrange ↔ green transitions do not change navigation logging
Stops on mission closeClosing the mission ends navigation logging immediately
The Record button starts and stops laser and imagery only. The trajectory records throughout the whole mission, including between runs.

DYNAMICS BEFORE, SMOOTHNESS DURING Initialization wants dynamics — they make errors observable. Collection wants the vehicle predictable. Every unmodelled jolt is a demand on the IMU and noise in the cloud.

BEST IN-VEHICLE QC CHECK TMI draws the trajectory as a thin blue line and recorded areas as a thick blue line. Every stretch you meant to collect should be thick. Finding a thin one here costs minutes; finding it in the office costs a mobilisation.

9   Useful during a mission SOP §9

  • Comments — time-tags notes into the mission database. Use it.
  • Waterfall view — confirms the laser is producing sensible data
  • Camera exposure — adjust live when lighting changes materially
  • Record only project-specific areas
  • Stop recording for turnarounds; restart on the new pass
  • Need different capture settings? Use mission re-configuration — do NOT close the mission (no re-initialization needed)

10   Ending the mission SOP §10

  • 1Stop recording the final run
  • 2Drive to an initialization point, dynamic manoeuvres on the way
  • 3FINALIZE SEQUENCE — mirror of the start:   dynamic steering → vary speed → drive straight → STILL 2–3 min
  • 4Review the map — thick blue line over every intended stretch
  • 5Close mission → Complete Mission
  • 6Shut down → Shutdown
  • 7Wait for power button light OUT — up to 90 seconds
  • 8Field protocol complete — runs, directions, date, mission, system S/N
  • 9Post-mission safety check (block 2)
NEVER PULL POWER Always use Complete Mission AND Shutdown, or risk corrupted data.  Do the map review BEFORE closing — if a run is missing and you are still on site and initialized, you can re-drive it.
WHY THE MIRROR Office software solves the trajectory forwards from the start and backwards from the end, then blends them. A good initialization at both ends means the weakest part — the middle — is solved from two strong anchors instead of one. It cannot be added later.

11   Data handling SOP §11

  • System fully shut down before removing disks
  • Unlock and remove BOTH SSDs — they are one dataset
  • Offload via both Data Carrier Docks, USB 3
  • Verify: navigation data · both lasers · panoramic · back-down camera · Mission Report · logs
  • Back up before any processing
  • Only then prepare SSDs for the next mission
TWO HARD RULES Never connect USB while a disk is in the Control Unit — remove it first.   Never delete raw mission data because processing succeeded or the deliverable shipped.
