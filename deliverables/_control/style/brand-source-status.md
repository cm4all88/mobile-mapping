# Brand source status

**Authority:** *Parametrix Brand Guide*, v6, November 2023, 32 pp.

Everything visual in the four MX60 documents is either **cited to a page of that guide** or marked
as an **extension** — a decision the guide does not make, built from brand values. This file is the
boundary between the two.

## The extensions, and why each exists

| Extension | Why the guide is silent | What it is built from |
|---|---|---|
| **Monospace typeface** | The guide names three faces, none monospaced | A system monospace stack. Used only for filenames, paths and software identifiers |
| **Table styling** | Not addressed | Charcoal header on Light Gray 4, hairline rules in Light Gray 3, no vertical rules, no striping — the "clarity and scanability" principle of p.9 applied |
| **Callout styling** | Only the p.16 quotation treatment exists | Its left-rule form, extended across ten callout types, coloured only from the palette |
| **Callout colour assignment** | Not addressed | Red for CAUTION and Calm Red for IMPORTANT, so the alert pair reads as one family at two levels. Greys for evidence. Tertiary pastels for the unsettled. Red stays sparing, per p.11 and p.23 |
| **Document identity accent** | Not addressed | One **secondary** colour per document. The guide states secondary colours are accents "useful for … enhancing categorization" (p.18) |
| **Screen and dark mode** | The guide is print and marketing | The knockout logo, which the guide supplies for dark and busy grounds (p.11). Palette hues held; only their roles swap |
| **Technical-report cover** | Only presentation and letter covers are published | The ix-formation cover archetype of p.25, at document proportions |

> **One caveat on the document accents.** Page 28 correlates secondary colours with sectors —
> green environmental, yellow transportation, blue water, orange community — in the context of one
> social-media template. These are internal technical documents and imply no sector. If that
> reading is a problem, the accents can be reassigned or dropped without touching anything else;
> they live in one token.

## What the brand guide does not decide

The guide is authoritative for visual identity. It says nothing about, and does not settle:

- controlled-document numbering or SOP identifiers
- revision-number conventions
- approval authorities
- effective-date rules
- retention requirements
- controlled-copy terminology

Those remain open Parametrix **document-control** decisions and are tracked in the master register,
not here. The style specification reserves space for them; it does not fill it.
