## The three states

Every clause carries one. **At this revision none is ADOPTED**, which is correct on a first issue
and is stated on the front matter.

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **ADOPTED** | Binding. Recorded in Appendix A with a date and an approver |
| **PARAMETRIX DECISION REQUIRED** | The requirement is identified; the answer is not set. Tracked against a **D-** identifier |
| **TESTING REQUIRED** | The requirement depends on a result nobody has obtained. Tracked against a **T-** or **V-** identifier, in the Technical Manual Appendix E |
